About Me

I'm Shuvankar Debnath — an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata (graduating 2027), working at the intersection of electrical systems, full-stack software development, and artificial intelligence.

My academic foundation in Electrical Engineering shapes how I reason about software: thinking in systems, feedback loops, stability, and control. In my independent work, I turn technical concepts into functional web applications, computer vision experiments, and embedded prototypes.

Through industry trainee exposure at ONGC (Oil and Natural Gas Corporation) and backend engineering internship at IEM Research Foundation, I have worked with industrial power equipment safety standards as well as production REST APIs and MongoDB schemas used by active institutional users.

In addition to engineering, years of freelance visual design (Photoshop, Illustrator) and video production (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve) enable me to design intuitive interfaces and present complex technical work clearly.
