About Me

Electrical Engineering × Software × AI

I explore the intersection of electrical engineering, software development, and artificial intelligence by building practical projects and learning through hands-on work.

I am Shuvankar Debnath, an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), affiliated with the University of Engineering & Management, Kolkata. I am interested in applying software and artificial intelligence to practical engineering problems.

My work spans electrical engineering, full-stack development, AI/ML, and embedded systems. I enjoy understanding how systems work and developing products that make technology more useful, accessible, or efficient. Alongside my academic learning, I independently build and improve projects across these areas—from an AI-enabled academic management and learning platform to computer-vision research, electric-vehicle control simulations, and ESP32-based automation.

I have also pursued industry-oriented training and technical exposure at organisations including ONGC, Durgapur Projects Limited (DPL), and the National Test House (Eastern Region). These experiences helped me connect engineering concepts with industrial equipment, testing practices, safety processes, and technical operations.

I approach each project as an opportunity to learn, experiment, and solve a defined problem. I value clear engineering, practical implementation, continuous improvement, and the ability to work across disciplines. I am particularly interested in opportunities where electrical engineering, intelligent systems, and software can complement one another.

How I Approach Projects

A systematic framework rooted in first-principles engineering and disciplined execution.

01
Understand the problem

Identify the purpose, users, constraints, and expected outcome before choosing a technology.

02
Learn by building

Develop a working prototype or implementation, then improve it through testing and iteration.

03
Connect disciplines

Look for opportunities to combine electrical engineering, software, and AI where it adds practical value.

04
Document the work

Keep project explanations, technical decisions, progress, and limitations understandable.

05
Be accurate

Distinguish completed implementations from prototypes, experiments, and ideas still in development.
