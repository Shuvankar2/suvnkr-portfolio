Graphics Designing Projects

A working archive of graphic design studies made while learning the craft — posters, social campaigns, logo explorations and layout experiments. Each project starts from a brief, a mood board and a palette, then moves through type hierarchy, composition and colour before it is exported.

These are learning pieces rather than client work, so they are deliberately varied: some test print-style typography, others explore social-first formats and brand marks. Click a piece to view it at full resolution.
