Graphics Designing Projects

A comprehensive archive of graphic design projects, brand identity studies, and marketing collateral developed across collegiate technical initiatives and freelance commissions. Each design begins with an intentional visual strategy: establishing a deliberate typographic hierarchy, calibrating high-contrast color palettes, and structuring layout geometry so key information communicates instantaneously.

My workflow combines vector-first ideation in Adobe Illustrator and Figma with high-fidelity raster composition and texture grading in Adobe Photoshop. Across promotional posters, social media campaign kits, and brand marks, I focus on balancing expressive aesthetics with strict functional legibility across varied screen sizes and physical print collateral.

These explorations span multiple formats: from multi-day event posters for automotive engineering workshops (SAE IEM Collegiate Club) to minimalist vector logos and social-first creative collateral. From vector pen tool drafting to color gamut conversions (CMYK for print production versus sRGB for digital displays), each asset is systematically exported with modern WebP and high-resolution raster formats to guarantee lightning-fast loading without visual degradation.

Every piece is optimized for rapid visual comprehension, color fidelity under varied display profiles, and crisp rendering across mobile and desktop environments.
