01What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to store. Similar technologies include localStorage, which keeps data until it is deleted, and sessionStorage, which is cleared when the tab closes. This policy uses "cookies" as shorthand for all of them.

Cookies can be first-party (set by this site) or third-party (set by another domain, such as Google or YouTube). They can be essential to make a site work, or optional, used for preferences, measurement or advertising.

02The categories I use

Category What it does Can you refuse it?
Strictly necessary Keeps the site working and remembers your consent choice. Without it the site cannot reliably respect your preferences. No — required to operate the site.
Preferences and embeds Remembers display choices and allows embedded video and document players to load. Yes.
Analytics Would help measure which pages are useful. No analytics product is currently installed on suvnkr.in. Yes.
Advertising Lets Google AdSense serve, cap and personalise ads on ad-supported product subdomains. Yes.

03Your consent and how to change it

On your first visit a banner asks whether to accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose per category. Nothing optional loads before you choose. Your decision is stored in your browser under the key suvnkr_consent_v1 so I do not ask on every page.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time:

  • use the link in the footer of any page;
  • clear this site's data in your browser settings, which resets the banner; or
  • use the consent controls provided by Google on ad-supported subdomains.

Withdrawing consent stops future optional processing but does not affect processing that already happened lawfully.

04Storage set by this site

Name Type Purpose Duration
suvnkr_consent_v1 localStorage Stores your cookie choice, its version and the time it was made. Until cleared or superseded
suvnkr_ai_session sessionStorage Keeps the AI assistant transcript while you move between pages. Used only when you use the assistant. 1 hour of inactivity, or when the tab closes

This site sets no first-party analytics or advertising cookies. I do not use fingerprinting or cross-site tracking of my own.

05Advertising cookies on product subdomains

The portfolio at suvnkr.in does not carry ads. Product subdomains such as gotiprep.suvnkr.in may display Google AdSense ads. When ads are enabled and you have consented where required, Google and its partners may set cookies including:

Cookie Set by Purpose Typical duration
IDE Google DoubleClick Measures ad interactions and prevents showing the same ad too often. Up to 13 months
__gads, __gpi Google Ad delivery, frequency capping and reporting. Up to 13 months
test_cookie Google DoubleClick Checks whether your browser accepts cookies. 15 minutes

Cookie names and durations are set by Google and may change. See Google's cookie documentation and the partner-sites notice. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalised ads and their cookies are only used after consent collected by a Google-certified consent platform.

06Embedded media and documents

Some pages embed YouTube and Vimeo videos and Google Drive previews. When an embed loads, the provider may set its own cookies and receive your IP address and device information, even if you do not press play. Providers commonly use:

  • YouTube: VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE, YSC and PREF, used for playback preferences and abuse prevention.
  • Vimeo: vuid, used to measure video plays.
  • Google Drive: Google account and preference cookies such as NID and SID, depending on whether you are signed in.

These embeds fall under the preferences-and-embeds category. If you reject that category, avoid playing embedded media; the rest of the site continues to work.

07Fonts and icon libraries

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts and icons from the Font Awesome library hosted on cdnjs (Cloudflare). These are direct requests to those providers and may expose your IP address and user agent in their logs. They do not set advertising cookies on this site. Blocking them may change how the site looks but will not stop it working.

08Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you view, block and delete cookies. Useful starting points:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking all cookies may break parts of the site, including the consent memory and embedded players.

09Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. Where a legally binding signal such as GPC applies, I treat it as a request to disable advertising and analytics cookies, and the consent manager reflects that by keeping those categories off. Because the portfolio currently loads no analytics or advertising scripts, no additional action is needed on suvnkr.in itself.

10Changes to this policy

I update this policy when the cookies I use or the providers I rely on change. The effective date at the top of the page shows the current version. Material changes will be announced on the site before they take effect.
