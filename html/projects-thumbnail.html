Thumbnail Projects

Thumbnail design is the most compressed form of visual storytelling: one frame has to communicate the subject, the emotion and the reason to click, often at 320 pixels wide. I have been designing thumbnails since 2020 for creators across gaming, education, tech reviews and vlogs.

Each piece below is built around three constraints — a readable focal point at small sizes, a limited palette that survives compression, and type that stays legible on a phone. Click any image to open the full-resolution version.
