01Who we are and what this policy covers

Shuvankar Debnath (SUVNKR) is an independent software and AI engineer based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. I operate the portfolio at suvnkr.in, the product subdomains under *.suvnkr.in, and the products published on them. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" refer to Shuvankar Debnath, and "you" refers to anyone who visits or uses those sites.

This policy applies to:

  • suvnkr.in — this portfolio, which does not display advertising.
  • gotiprep.suvnkr.in — the GotiPrep examination practice platform.
  • Kagazly — the browser-based document utility described on the products page.
  • Any future product, tool or subdomain that I publish under the suvnkr.in domain family, unless that product publishes its own privacy notice.

Where a product needs rules that differ from this one, that product will link to a product-specific notice and this policy will say so. Otherwise, this document is the single privacy reference for everything I run.

02The short version

  • I do not sell your personal data, and I do not run advertising on the portfolio at suvnkr.in.
  • Some product subdomains may show ads through Google AdSense. Where the law requires it, personalised ads only load after you consent through a Google-certified consent platform.
  • I do not currently run a web analytics product on this site.
  • The AI assistant on the portfolio sends your chat messages to a Cloudflare Worker and a third-party language-model provider so it can answer. Do not paste sensitive information into it.
  • Kagazly processes documents inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded to me.
  • You can change or withdraw cookie consent at any time using the control in the footer.

03Information I collect

Information you give me directly

  • Contact details. If you email me, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything else in your message. The site itself has no server-side contact form; your email client sends the message.
  • Account details. Products that offer accounts ask for a display name, email address and password (stored only as a salted hash by the authentication provider). Some products may also store a username, avatar or preferences.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by third-party payment processors. I receive a transaction reference, the amount, the currency, the plan and your billing email. I never receive or store your full card number, CVV or bank credentials.
  • Content you submit. Files, text, answers or other material you upload or create in a product. For Kagazly, processing happens locally in your browser and the file does not reach my servers.
  • AI assistant messages. The prompts you type into the portfolio assistant, along with the last few turns of that conversation.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical request data. My hosting and edge providers record IP address, user agent, requested URL, referrer, timestamp and response status in standard server logs for security and reliability.
  • Local storage. Your consent choice and, during a visit, the AI chat transcript are stored in your browser. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Advertising data. On ad-supported product subdomains, Google and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and browsing signals to measure and personalise ads, subject to your consent choices.

I do not intentionally collect special-category data (health, religion, biometric identifiers, precise location) and I ask you not to send it to me.

05The AI assistant

The portfolio includes an assistant that answers questions about my work. When you send a message, your browser posts the message, up to the last eight turns of the conversation, the current page title and your local time to a Cloudflare Worker that I control. That worker forwards the request to a third-party language-model provider to generate a reply.

  • Messages are transmitted over HTTPS and are used only to produce the reply.
  • I do not use your messages to train models, and I do not build advertising profiles from them.
  • The transcript is kept in your browser's sessionStorage so it survives navigation between pages, and it expires after one hour of inactivity or when you close the tab.
  • Third-party model providers may retain request logs for a limited period under their own policies. I select providers that offer no-training terms where available.
  • The assistant is a convenience, not an authoritative source. Do not enter passwords, financial details, identity documents, health information or anything else confidential.

06Cookies and similar technologies

I use browser storage for the consent choice and, during a visit, for the AI chat transcript. On product subdomains, Google AdSense and embedded media may set cookies after you consent. The full list, with names, purposes and durations, is in the Cookie Policy.

You can accept, reject or customise non-essential cookies in the banner, and change the choice later from the link in the footer of every page.

07Advertising and Google AdSense

The portfolio at suvnkr.in does not display ads. Advertising may appear on product subdomains such as gotiprep.suvnkr.in, which are separate properties within the same AdSense account.

Where ads are shown, they are served by Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-3063786907196879). Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to these sites or other sites.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Personalised advertising requires consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR. Consent is collected through a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Elsewhere. Ads may be personalised where local law permits, and you can opt out through Google's ad settings at any time.
  • Your controls. Manage or withdraw consent with the control, review My Ad Center, or opt out of personalised advertising from participating companies via aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

I do not control the cookies that Google or its partners set, and their handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

09When information is shared

I do not sell personal data. I share it only:

  • with the service providers above, to the extent needed to run the sites and products;
  • with payment processors and fraud-prevention providers to complete and protect transactions;
  • with professional advisers, auditors or insurers where necessary and under confidentiality;
  • with authorities when required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect rights, safety and security;
  • as part of a reorganisation, merger or transfer of the products, in which case the recipient remains bound by this policy for data already collected.

Aggregated, de-identified statistics (for example, page-view counts) may be published or shared freely because they do not identify you.

10International data transfers

I am based in India and most providers I use are in the United States or the European Union. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country. Where I transfer personal data out of the EEA, UK or Switzerland, I rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, together with the provider's own transfer mechanisms. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies, transfers are made consistent with its requirements.

11How long information is kept

  • Emails and enquiries: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted unless there is an ongoing matter.
  • Account data: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure, except records that must be kept for tax, fraud or legal reasons.
  • Uploaded content: for as long as you keep it in the product, then deleted within 30 days. Kagazly files never leave your browser.
  • Payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, generally eight years in India.
  • Server logs: typically 30 days or less at the hosting and edge providers.
  • AI chat transcript: in your browser only, expiring after one hour of inactivity or when the tab closes; provider-side logs follow the provider's retention period.
  • Consent record: until you clear it or it is superseded by a new consent version.

12How information is protected

All sites are served over HTTPS. Administrative access is protected by strong authentication and least-privilege rules. Passwords are stored as salted hashes by the authentication provider, never in plain text. Payment card data goes directly to the payment processor and never touches my systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to result in a risk to your rights, I will notify the relevant authority and affected users without undue delay, as required by applicable law.

13Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data, subject to legal retention duties;
  • restrict or object to processing, including profiling for direct marketing;
  • data portability, where processing is based on consent or contract;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising (CCPA/ CPRA);
  • appeal a decision or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

To exercise a right, email shuvankar345@gmail.com with enough detail for me to locate your data. I respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. I will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

14Children's privacy

The sites and products are intended for people aged 16 and over. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. Where local law sets a higher age for valid consent, that higher age applies. If you believe a child has provided personal data, email me and I will delete it promptly. Some paid features may require you to be 18 or to have a guardian's authorisation.
