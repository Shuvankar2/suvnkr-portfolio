Who am I

Hi, I am Shuvankar Debnath (SUVNKR) — a Software & AI Engineer and the maintainer of this website at shuvankar.qzz.io. This page informs you of the policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use this website and the choices you have associated with that data.

What personal data we collect and why

Contact

This website does not host a server-side contact form. When you use email links, your email client handles the data. I will only use your contact details to respond to your inquiry.


Embedded Content

Pages on this site include embedded content (e.g. YouTube videos, Vimeo embeds). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor visited those websites directly. These third-party sites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content.


Media / Images

Avoid uploading images with embedded location data (EXIF GPS). Visitors may be able to download and extract any location data from images on this website.

Cookies

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the website to function. They include your cookie consent preference stored locally so we do not ask you again. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies (Optional): Google Analytics may set cookies to help us understand how visitors interact with the site. These are only placed if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Advertising Cookies (Optional): Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. These are only placed if you accept advertising cookies.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites. Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Ads Settings.


You can opt out of performance/analytics tracking via:

  • Google Analytics: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Google AdSense: google.com/ads/preferences/html/opt-out.html

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to track and monitor usage of this website. Google uses the collected data to contextualize and personalize the ads of its own advertising network. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. For more information, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties, except as required by law or through the third-party services listed in this policy (Google Analytics, Google AdSense).

How long we retain your data

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data processed by third-party services (Google Analytics, AdSense) is subject to their own retention policies. Review Google's data retention at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your rights over your data

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent to optional cookies at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting this site to update your preferences.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Your information

You should not provide any sensitive personal information on this website. If you choose to contact me via email, only share what is necessary for your inquiry.
